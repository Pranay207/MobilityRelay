# Fictional Task Validation

This measures a coordination task, not repair speed or health outcomes. Use only
fictional records. Obtain consent, do not recruit by disclosing private records,
and let the participant stop without explanation. A student/caregiver proxy test
is not a wheelchair-user outcome study; a self-test is not independent validation.

## Task

Give the participant the two fictional dated supplier/insurer reports in Maya's
case. Ask them to identify missing receipt information, prepare a supported
follow-up without blaming either party, schedule a next check, and hand over an
attributed history. Do not explain which buttons to use during the timed task.

Compare the task with ordinary notes/email using a different, equivalent
fictional case. Alternate the order across participants to reduce learning bias.
Do not actually email a supplier or insurer. Capture mode must remain visible.

Record completion, elapsed time, wrong recipient/source/date, unsupported claims,
missed review, and requests for assistance. Ask which part was confusing and
whether the resulting case pack would be useful. Record failures and objections,
not only compliments. Do not publish identifying recordings without permission.

size, task setup and limitations. Do not infer population percentages, cost
savings, fewer missed appointments, faster repairs or medical benefits.

If nobody is available, document a self-walkthrough and leave independent-user
validation marked incomplete. This is more credible than an invented testimonial.
